# DM2 Schedule

Class timetable, attendance and deadlines app for Great Lakes DM2. Works on Android and iPhone, and keeps working without internet.

## The app link

### 👉 https://dwaipayanmahalanobis.github.io/dm2-schedule/

**You do not download any file from this page.** The files listed above are the app's code. GitHub uses them to run the website at the link above. To get the app, open that link on your phone and follow the steps below.

---

## Android phone (Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Pixel, etc.)

1. Open **Chrome** on your phone (the round red, yellow and green icon).
2. Tap the address bar at the top, type `dwaipayanmahalanobis.github.io/dm2-schedule` and press Enter. You can also send yourself the link on WhatsApp and tap it; if it opens inside WhatsApp, tap ⋮ and choose **Open in Chrome**.
3. The DM2 Schedule welcome screen appears. **Wait about 10 seconds** while the app saves itself to your phone.
4. Tap the **three dots ⋮** at the top right of Chrome.
5. Tap **Install app**. (On some phones it says **Add to Home screen**.)
6. Tap **Install** (or **Add**) in the box that pops up.
7. The DM2 Schedule icon (Great Lakes logo) appears on your home screen or in your app drawer. **From now on, always open it from that icon**, not from Chrome.

## iPhone

1. Open **Safari** (the blue compass icon). **It must be Safari.** Chrome on iPhone cannot add apps to the home screen properly.
2. Tap the address bar, type `dwaipayanmahalanobis.github.io/dm2-schedule` and tap **Go**. If you got the link on WhatsApp and it opens inside WhatsApp, tap the Safari/compass button to open it in Safari.
3. Wait about 10 seconds on the welcome screen.
4. Tap the **Share button**: the square with an arrow pointing up. It is at the bottom of the screen (or at the top right on some iPhones). If you can't see the bottom bar, scroll up a little or tap the bottom of the screen.
5. Scroll down the list that opens and tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you don't see it, scroll to the very bottom, tap **Edit Actions…**, and add it.
6. Tap **Add** at the top right.
7. The DM2 Schedule icon appears on your home screen. **From now on, always open it from that icon**, not from Safari.

## First-time setup inside the app

1. Open the app from its new icon.
2. Type your name and a sync key (any word you'll remember, e.g. `rohit-pgdm`), then tap **Create Key**.
3. Add your timetable in one of two ways:
   - **Fresh start:** tap the **sliders icon** (top right) → **Select Timetable Excel** → pick the Excel file (from Downloads, Files, Drive or WhatsApp documents).
   - **Bring your existing attendance from the laptop:** on the laptop, open the app → sliders icon → **Save Backup**. This makes a `.json` file. Send that file to your phone (WhatsApp to yourself, email or Drive) and save it. Then in the phone app: sliders icon → **Restore** → pick that file.

## Good to know

- **Works without internet.** After the first open, everything (the app, your timetable, your attendance) is stored on your phone.
- **Your data stays on your phone.** Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Marks you make on the phone don't appear on the laptop by themselves, and laptop marks don't appear on the phone. To move data between them, use **Save Backup** on one device and **Restore** on the other.
- **Don't delete the app or clear Chrome/Safari data** without first making a backup (sliders icon → Save Backup). Deleting the app deletes the attendance stored in it.
- **Updates arrive automatically.** When the app is changed, the new version loads the next time you open the app while online. Your data is kept.
- **Excel export on the phone** opens the share menu, so you can save the sheet to Files or Drive, or send it on WhatsApp.

## Something not working?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No "Install app" option on Android | Make sure you are in **Chrome**, not inside WhatsApp or another browser. Reload the page, wait 10 seconds, try ⋮ again. |
| No "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone | Make sure you are in **Safari**. In the Share list, scroll down or use **Edit Actions…** |
| Icons look like empty boxes | Open the app once with internet so it can finish saving itself. |
| The app opened but my data is gone | You probably opened the website in Chrome/Safari instead of the home-screen icon. Each has its own storage, so use the icon. |
