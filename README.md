## 1. Install on Mobile

### Android phone (Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Pixel, etc.)

1. Open **Chrome** on your phone (the round red, yellow and green icon).
2. Tap the address bar at the top, type `dwaipayanmahalanobis.github.io/dm2-schedule` and press Enter. You can also send yourself the link on WhatsApp and tap it; if it opens inside WhatsApp, tap **⋮** and choose **Open in Chrome**.
3. The DM2 Schedule welcome screen appears. **Wait about 10 seconds** while the app saves itself to your phone.
4. Tap the **three dots ⋮** at the top right of Chrome.
5. Tap **Install app**. (On some phones it says **Add to Home screen**.)
6. Tap **Install** (or **Add**) in the box that pops up.
7. The **DM2 Schedule** icon (Great Lakes logo) appears on your home screen or in your app drawer. **From now on, always open it from that icon**, not from Chrome.

### iPhone

1. Open **Safari** (the blue compass icon). **It must be Safari.** Chrome on iPhone cannot add apps to the home screen properly.
2. Tap the address bar, type `dwaipayanmahalanobis.github.io/dm2-schedule` and tap **Go**. If you got the link on WhatsApp and it opens inside WhatsApp, tap the Safari/compass button to open it in Safari.
3. **Wait about 10 seconds** on the welcome screen.
4. Tap the **Share button**: the square with an arrow pointing up. It is at the bottom of the screen (or at the top right on some iPhones). If you can't see the bottom bar, tap near the bottom of the screen.
5. Scroll down the list that opens and tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you don't see it, scroll to the very bottom, tap **Edit Actions…**, and add it.
6. Tap **Add** at the top right.
7. The **DM2 Schedule** icon appears on your home screen. **From now on, always open it from that icon**, not from Safari.

Next: read [The one rule that matters most](#3-the-one-rule-that-matters-most), then do [First-time setup on Mobile](#4-first-time-setup-on-mobile).

## 3. The one rule that matters most

The app shows **only the classes of the courses you add under My Courses**. It finds your classes by matching each course's **name** and **section** against the timetable Excel. So when you add a course:

- **Course name:** type it **exactly as it is written in the Excel**, with the same spelling, same words and same spacing. Example: if the Excel says `Options, Futures and Derivatives`, do **not** type `Options Futures & Derivatives` or `OFD`.
- **Professor name:** type it **exactly as it appears in the Excel** too (for example `Prof. Priya Natarajan`). It is shown on your class cards and lets you search for a class by professor.
- **Section:** pick the **same section** the Excel shows for your class (Section 1, 2, 3 or 4).
- **Total lectures:** the total number of sessions in the whole course (usually 10 or 20). The app uses this to work out how many classes you can miss. 80% attendance is mandatory, so 10 lectures = 2 misses allowed and 20 lectures = 4.

> **Easiest way to get names exactly right:** import your Excel **first**. The app then lists every course it found in your timetable under **"Found in your timetable but not in your list"**. Tapping one adds it with the exact name and professor from the Excel, so you only need to check the section and total lectures.

## 4. First-time setup on Mobile

Do this once, inside the app you installed (open it from its **home-screen icon**).

**Step 1: Create your workspace**
1. On the welcome screen, type **Your Name**.
2. In **Workspace Sync Key**, type any word you will remember (letters, numbers and dashes only, e.g. `rohit-pgdm`). Or tap **Generate Random Sync Key**.
3. Tap **Create Key**.

**Step 2: Import your timetable Excel**
1. First save the timetable Excel on your phone. In WhatsApp, email or Drive, open the file and download/save it. On **iPhone**, save it to the **Files** app (open the file → Share → **Save to Files**).
2. In the app, tap the **sliders icon** (top right, next to the 🔍).
3. Tap **Select Timetable Excel**, find the file (Downloads, Files, Drive or WhatsApp documents) and tap it.
4. The week is saved automatically under the Excel's sheet name (you'll see it in **Saved Week** at the top). Close the panel with the **✕**.

**Step 3: Set up your courses (most important)**
1. Tap **Courses** in the bottom bar.
2. The list starts with some **sample courses**. For each course you **do not** take, tap the **red bin icon** on its card and confirm.
3. Scroll down to **"Found in your timetable but not in your list"** and tap it to open the list. Tap each course **you take** to add it with the exact name and professor from the Excel.
4. For **every** course now in your list, tap the **blue pencil icon** on its card. The form at the bottom of the page fills in. Check that:
   - **Course name** and **Professor** match the Excel **exactly** (same spelling, same spacing).
   - **Section** is your section.
   - **Total lectures** is correct.

   Then tap the green **Save** button.
5. If a course you take is **not** in the "Found" list, add it with the form at the bottom: type the **name** and **professor exactly as in the Excel**, choose the **section**, enter **total lectures**, pick a **colour**, and tap **Add**.

**Step 4: Check it worked**
1. Tap **Today** in the bottom bar. You should see only your own classes.
2. If a class you take is missing, its name or section in My Courses doesn't match the Excel. Go back to Step 3 and fix the spelling, spacing or section.

> **Already using the app on another device?** You can skip Steps 2–3 and bring everything over instead. On the other device: sliders icon → **Save Backup**. Send that `.json` file to your phone (WhatsApp to yourself, email or Drive) and save it. Then in this app: sliders icon → **Restore** → choose that file.

## 5. First-time setup on PC

Do this once, in the browser (or installed app) from [Open on PC](#2-open-on-pc).

**Step 1: Create your workspace**
1. On the welcome screen, type **Your Name**.
2. In **Workspace Sync Key**, type any word you will remember (letters, numbers and dashes only, e.g. `rohit-pgdm`). Or click **Generate Random Sync Key**.
3. Click **Create Key**.

**Step 2: Import your timetable Excel**
1. Save the timetable Excel somewhere on your PC (e.g. Downloads).
2. Click **Import** at the top right. Or click the **sliders icon** → **Select Timetable Excel**, or drag the Excel file onto the dashed box in that panel.
3. Choose the Excel file. The week is saved automatically under the Excel's sheet name (you'll see it in **Saved Week** at the top).

**Step 3: Set up your courses (most important)**
1. Click the **My Courses** tab at the top.
2. The list starts with some **sample courses**. For each course you **do not** take, click the **red bin icon** on its card and confirm.
3. Below the list, click **"Found in your timetable but not in your list"** to open it. Click each course **you take** to add it with the exact name and professor from the Excel.
4. For **every** course now in your list, click the **blue pencil icon** on its card. The form at the bottom of the page fills in. Check that:
   - **Course name** and **Professor** match the Excel **exactly** (same spelling, same spacing). Tip: copy the text from the Excel cell (`Ctrl + C`) and paste it (`Ctrl + V`).
   - **Section** is your section.
   - **Total lectures** is correct.

   Then click the green **Save** button.
5. If a course you take is **not** in the "Found" list, add it with the form at the bottom: type or paste the **name** and **professor exactly as in the Excel**, choose the **section**, enter **total lectures**, pick a **colour**, and click **Add**.

**Step 4: Check it worked**
1. Click the **Today** tab. You should see only your own classes.
2. If a class you take is missing, its name or section in My Courses doesn't match the Excel. Go back to Step 3 and fix the spelling, spacing or section.

> **Already using the app on your phone?** You can skip Steps 2–3. In the phone app: sliders icon → **Save Backup**, get the `.json` file onto your PC. Then here: sliders icon → **Restore** → choose that file.

## 6. Daily use

| What you want | Mobile | PC |
|---|---|---|
| See today's classes | **Today** in the bottom bar | **Today** tab |
| See another day | Tap the day in the strip at the top of Today | Same |
| Mark attendance | On each class, tap **Mark Attended**, **Absent**, **On Duty (OD)** or **Class cancelled / Rescheduled** | Same buttons on each class card |
| Undo a mark | Tap the same button again | Same |
| Add a reason for OD | Tap **On Duty (OD)**; a box opens for an optional reason | Same |
| Find a class or professor | Tap **🔍** at the top | Click **Find** at the top (or press `/`) |
| See all dates of a course with its attendance | 🔍 → type the course or professor → tap the course | Same |
| Add next week's timetable | Sliders icon → **Select Timetable Excel** | **Import** button |
| Switch between weeks | **Saved Week** drop-down at the top | Same |
| Export attendance to Excel | Sliders icon → **Export Attendance (Excel)** | **Export** button at the top |
| Add a deadline | **Deadlines** in the bottom bar | **Deadlines** tab |
| Back up your data | Sliders icon → **Save Backup** | Same |

**What each mark means**
- **Attended:** counts as present.
- **Absent:** counts as a miss (uses up one of your allowed misses).
- **On Duty (OD):** counts as present and **does not** use up a miss.
- **Class cancelled / Rescheduled:** the session is **not counted at all**. When you import the updated Excel, the class appears at its new date, unmarked.

## 7. Good to know

- **Works without internet.** After the first open, everything (the app, your timetable, your attendance) is stored on your device.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your phone and PC do **not** sync by themselves: marks made on one don't appear on the other. To copy everything across, use **Save Backup** on one device and **Restore** on the other.
- **Make a backup now and then** (sliders icon → **Save Backup**). If you delete the app, clear your browser's data, or change phones, your attendance is lost unless you have a backup.
- **Updates arrive automatically.** When the app is improved, the new version loads the next time you open it while online. Your data is kept.
- **Excel export on mobile** opens the share menu, so you can save the sheet to Files or Drive, or send it on WhatsApp.

## 8. Something not working?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| A class I take does not show up | The course **name** or **section** in My Courses doesn't match the Excel. Fix the spelling/spacing (copy-paste it from the Excel), or add the course from **"Found in your timetable"**. |
| Classes I don't take are showing | Remove those courses from My Courses (red bin icon). |
| "Can miss" number looks wrong | Edit the course (blue pencil) and set the correct **Total lectures**. |
| No "Install app" option on Android | Make sure you are in **Chrome**, not inside WhatsApp or another browser. Reload the page, wait 10 seconds, try **⋮** again. |
| No "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone | Make sure you are in **Safari**. In the Share list, scroll down or use **Edit Actions…** |
| Can't pick the Excel file on iPhone | Save the Excel to the **Files** app first (in WhatsApp: open the file → Share → **Save to Files**), then pick it from Files. |
| Icons look like empty boxes | Open the app once with internet so it can finish saving itself. |
| The app opened but my data is gone | You probably opened the website in the browser instead of the installed icon (or a different browser on PC). Each keeps its own data, so always use the same one. |
